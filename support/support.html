GOOD TO KNOW

Quick answers

What should I write in a support request?

Briefly describe what is happening and what kind of help you are looking for. You can keep personal details limited.

Can I book directly with a counsellor?

Yes. Choose a counsellor above or open Appointments to select a concern, date and time.

What if I need help urgently?

If there is immediate danger, contact emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department. You can also reach out to someone you trust nearby.
